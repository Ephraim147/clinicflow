# Problem Discovery: ClinicFlow

## 1. The Problem (2 sentences)
Small clinics lose money and patient time when appointment slots go unused
because patients don't show up. Receptionists also manage bookings by phone
and paper, which leads to double-bookings, mistakes, and long waits.

## 2. Who Is Affected (5 people)
| Person | What they want from the clinic system |
|---|---|
| Patient | Book a slot easily, get a reminder, avoid long waiting |
| Receptionist | Book quickly, see the doctor's schedule clearly, avoid double-bookings |
| Doctor | A clear daily schedule with fewer empty slots and fewer surprises |
| Clinic owner | Fewer missed appointments, better use of doctors' time, more revenue |
| Data privacy / compliance person | Patient data kept safe and only seen by the right people |

## 3. How Clinics Manage Appointments Today
Three ways:
1. Paper diary with phone call bookings
2. WhatsApp messages to the receptionist
3. Excel or Google Sheets / Google Calendar

Two real clinic software products:
1. Practo (appointment booking and clinic management)
2. Cliniko or Jane App (clinic scheduling software)

## 4. Pain Points (5 specific problems)
1. Two patients arrive for the same time slot because of a booking mistake.
2. A patient forgets the appointment, and the doctor's slot stays empty.
3. The receptionist is busy on calls all day and can't answer new callers.
4. The doctor's availability changes at short notice, and patients are not told.
5. Patients wait a long time because there is no way to see how busy the clinic is.
